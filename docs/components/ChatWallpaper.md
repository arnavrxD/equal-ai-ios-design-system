# ChatWallpaper

The doodle wallpaper behind the Equal chat sheet: `#E8E8E8` with the doodle pattern (`EqualDoodlePattern`) in black at 32% inside a 16% layer, about 5% ink. The art tiles vertically so tall sheets stay covered.

- Variants: `.full` (fills its frame, 392×446 in the sheet) and `.strip` (165pt band, "BG pattern 6").
- Consumer passes: the style and an optional layer `opacity` (0.16).
- Do place it under the thread only, below the white sheet header.
- Don't raise the opacity: the bubbles must stay the focus.
