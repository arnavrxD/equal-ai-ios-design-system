# QuickActionTile

Shortcut tile. **round**: 72pt white circle with a green glyph and a 12pt label (Message, Discover, Call, Tell Equal). **square**: 108×98 white card (radius 16, no stroke, `0 0 4 #000@16%` shadow), 36pt green glyph, 11pt black label (Talk to Equal, Set Instruction, Make a Call); it narrows to 98 when space is tight.

- Consumer passes: title, `glyph` (`.icon` or `.equalMark`), `style`, `action`.
- Do use three square tiles in the Equal sheet.
- Don't mix round and square tiles in one row.
