# MembershipCard

"Equal GOLD" card in Settings and on the subscription page: black with `gradient-membership`, a gold glow, "Equal" + gradient "GOLD" wordmark, a status Tag (Active / Free Trial / Expired) and one line of detail; a price column on the subscription page, a chevron in Settings, a yellow Renew button when expired.

- Props: `status` active | trial | expired, `subtitle`, `price`.
