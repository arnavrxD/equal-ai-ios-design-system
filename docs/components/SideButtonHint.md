# SideButtonHint

"Double click to Subscribe": a 6×106 white bar that pulses against the right screen edge (next to the iPhone side button) with SemiBold 24 white text beside it. Shown over the dimmed screen while `EqualPurchaseConfirmSheet` is up.

- Pass the text (two lines by default). The pulse respects Reduce Motion.
- Do place it top-trailing, 6pt from the edge, about 147pt from the top.
- Don't show it without a dimmed background.
