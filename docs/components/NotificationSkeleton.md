# NotificationSkeleton

A placeholder lock-screen notification (245×52, radius 16.4): frosted white-50% card with a 36pt grey icon square and three bars (`#CCCDC8`, `#D9D9D9`). Two stack 21pt apart under the real notification in `EqualPermissionPreview`.

- Variants: `.primary` (opacity 1), `.faded` (layer opacity 0.5).
- Consumer passes: the emphasis only.
- Do place it over the lock-screen wallpaper so the frost reads.
- Don't use it as a loading state for app content.
