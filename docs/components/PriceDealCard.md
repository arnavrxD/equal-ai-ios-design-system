# PriceDealCard

Paywall-v2 deal card: the struck "₹ 499", a big gold DM Sans ExtraBold "₹249" whose digits roll from the old price on appear, "/month", an optional "☕ Less than your weekly cup of coffee" line, concentric rings with a gold glow, and a LIMITED TIME DEAL tag (alarm SF Symbol) straddling the top edge.

- Variants: full (122pt) and compact (`comparison: nil`, 99pt).
- Pass `price`, `originalPrice`, `period`, `comparison`, `tag`, `rollsOnAppear`.
- Do leave about 11pt above it for the tag.
- Figma uses Geist and outlined vector digits; this is DM Sans with `.numericText` transitions.
