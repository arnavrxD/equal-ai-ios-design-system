# SheetHeader

Top of the Equal chat sheet: 98pt white, an 89×7 `#ADADAD` handle 12pt from the top, the 28pt Equal dot (soft shadow), the task title (Regular 17 `#111`) and a 48pt white close button with a `#555` glyph, with a `0 4 16 #191919@8%` shadow onto the thread. The header is square; the sheet supplies the 28pt corners.

- Consumer passes: `title`, `onClose`.
- Do put it at the top of a sheet that clips to radius 28.
- Don't round it on its own.
