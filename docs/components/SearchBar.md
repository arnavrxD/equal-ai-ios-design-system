# SearchBar

Home search: a 52pt white pill with a 20pt search glyph and a placeholder that rolls vertically through "Search for names → numbers → categories → keywords" (every 2.2s, off with Reduce Motion), plus the 42pt glass ⋯ button 16pt to its right.

- Consumer passes: `query`, an optional fixed `placeholder` (turns the ticker off), `terms`, `showsMore`, `onMore`.
- Do keep the ticker words short.
- Don't animate it while the field has text.
