# StarHalo

Decoration behind the trial-started mascot: two concentric rounded 8-point star outlines (251 and 168, white 40%) with a white-to-gold glow (280, blur 154) sitting 74.5pt above their centre.

- Single state; scales with `size` (Figma 280).
- Do centre it on the mascot and put content on top in a ZStack.
- Don't use it on dark backgrounds; the white strokes disappear.
