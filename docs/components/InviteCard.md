# InviteCard

Referral card at the bottom of Settings (Figma 26242:90508): lime tint with a green glow, Black 16 "Invite a Friend", Regular 14 copy, a 36pt green "Share now" pill and the 3D high-five (tilted 6.4°). The card is 125pt tall and grows if the copy wraps.

- Props: `title`, `message`, `onShare`.
