# Avatar

Round caller avatar, 56px in the chat list (18px inside contact chips). Saved contacts show semibold initials in black-60% on `avatar-mint` with a small person badge; businesses show a 3D category illustration on a category tint (`avatar-finance`, `avatar-ride`, `avatar-career`, `avatar-peach`, `avatar-others`, `avatar-unknown`). Equal's own avatar is the six-dot mark on `gradient-voice`.

- Props: `size`, `initials` or `src`, `tint`, `contact` (person badge), `equal`.
