# SectionHeader

A list section header on the legacy home: "CONVERSATIONS" in DM Sans ExtraBold 14 `#8A8888` on the left and a 36pt glass ⋯ button (white 30%, `#F4F4F4` rim, soft 8% shadow) on the right, in a 36pt row.

- Variants: with or without the ⋯ button (`showsMore`).
- Consumer passes: `title` (uppercased for you) and `onMore`.
- Do use it above compact chat rows.
- Don't use it on paywalls; `EqualSectionLabel` covers the centred gold labels.
