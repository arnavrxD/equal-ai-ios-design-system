# PermissionPreview

The device preview on the permission intro: the iPhone 17 Pro Max frame (bundled image) over the blue lock screen (wallpaper, 25% black, DM Sans date and clock), faded into the page colour, with a frosted Equal notification.

- Variants: `.highlighted` (340×463: large notification breaking out of the phone plus two skeletons) and `.stacked` (345×515: bigger phone with the small "Missed call from Mom" notification, used behind the system alerts).
- Consumer passes: `title`, `message` and the `fade` colour (white, or `#F7F7F7` on grey pages).
- Do keep it fixed-size and centred.
- Don't put interactive content on it: it is one accessibility element.
