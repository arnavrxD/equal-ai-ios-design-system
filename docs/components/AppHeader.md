# AppHeader

Home top bar on `bg-surface` with a `stroke-subtle` bottom rule: the equal ai wordmark, the plan Tag (GOLD / GOLD PLUS) when subscribed, and on the right either the AvailabilityPill, the TrialPill (last trial days) or the user's avatar (before a plan).

- Props: `plan`, `right` availability | trial | avatar, `trialText`.
