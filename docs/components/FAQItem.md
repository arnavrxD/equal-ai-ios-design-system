# FAQItem

Accordion row: question in medium 16px, a grey caret that flips when open, answer in 14px `text-secondary`. Rows sit in a white `radius-l` group with hairline separators, followed by a "Still have questions?" support link.

- Props: `question`, `answer`, `open`. `EqualDS.FAQ` takes `items` [{q, a}] and `firstOpen`.
