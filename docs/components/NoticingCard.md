# NoticingCard

"Equal is noticing…" card under About You in Settings (Figma 26242:89472, 345×204): a `#F8F8F8` radius-16 card with a white neural-network line drawing (86 hairline connections, 37 nodes with a soft `#E5EBFF` glow, drawn with `Canvas`), green and lime blur blobs, a header row with the Equal dot and a faint share button, two lines of copy and the mascot with floating feature cards (`EqualImage.mascotFeaturesCards`, the only bitmap).

- States: one; a light-only surface (it forces the light colour scheme).
- The consumer passes `title`, `message` (defaults are the Figma copy) and `onShare`.
- Do keep the copy to two short lines within 184pt; the mascot fills the right half.
- Don't put it inside a settings group or add a chevron; it sits above the About You rows with a 12pt gap.
